  • Network information (connection type, provider)

2.2 Application Data

  • Language preferences (Spanish/English)
  • Application settings (exercise preferences)

2.3 Device Permissions

9. Minors and Age Eligibility

Breath Exercises is intended for users 18 years of age or older. The app is distributed on Google Play with an age restriction that does not allow users under 18 to download or install it.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a user under 18 has provided us with information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

On Android, we may use the Google Play Age Signals API to help provide an age-appropriate experience where required by applicable laws. This service is provided by Google Play and may return information such as whether a user is verified as an adult, under parental supervision, or whether access has been restricted. We use this information only to:

  • Determine whether the app may be used or whether access should be limited;
  • Decide whether advertising may be shown, in line with age-appropriate experience requirements;
  • Comply with legal and platform obligations.

We do not use Play Age Signals data for advertising targeting, user profiling, or marketing purposes. We do not store age signal data on our own servers; it is processed at runtime through Google Play services.

Where the Play Age Signals API is unavailable or does not return age information, we apply conservative rules designed to protect users and comply with applicable requirements.
